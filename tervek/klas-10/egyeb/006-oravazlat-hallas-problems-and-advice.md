## Lesson plan. Listening. Problems and Advice

**Name**: Valentyna Dudik **Date**: **Length**: 45 min **Class size** **Level**: B1

**Main aim**: To develop students' skills of listening for gist in the context of an interview where a journalist asks A man about his nasty experience of flying; To develop students' skills of listening for specific information in the context of the interview.

**Secondary**

To better enable students to discuss topics related to the man's experience and his survival;

To develop students' fluency in the context of discussing Aaron's experience.

**Materials & equipment required**

Empower B1 by Adrian Doff, Craig Thaine, Pre-Intermediate, Student's book, Teacher's book, Cambridge University Press, 2015

**Assumptions about the learners**

I expect students to know some lexical units on the topic of "Problems and Advice"; I expect students to be willing to discuss this challenging experience and what they think about similar experiences.

**Professional development goals** I will not overuse with the CCQs. I will focus on feedback accuracy, lower TTT and time limit.

**Professional development stages** I have planned my stages in such a way that the students will have enough time to work in pairs in breakout rooms working together. I will change the partners.
