## Yearly English Study Plan - Level A2

## 1. Grammar

- Present Simple vs. Present Continuous
- Past Simple: Regular and Irregular Verbs
- Future Forms: Going to and Will
- Articles: A, An, The
- Prepositions of Place and Time
- Comparative and Superlative Adjectives

## 2. Vocabulary

- Daily Routine and Activities
- Food and Drink
- Travel and Holidays
- Weather and Seasons
- Jobs and Professions
- Shopping and Money

## 3. Reading

- Read short stories and simplified books.
- Practice reading comprehension with questions.
- Learn to skim texts for main ideas.

## 4. Listening

- Listen to simple podcasts or dialogues.
- Watch videos with subtitles.
- Practice listening for specific information.

## 5. Speaking

- Practice introducing yourself and talking about daily life.
- Role-play common scenarios (e.g., shopping, ordering food).
- Join a speaking club or find a language partner.

## 6. Writing

- Write short paragraphs about your day or a recent trip.
- Practice writing emails or messages.
- Focus on using proper sentence structure.

## 7. Tips for Success

- Study for 20-30 minutes daily.
- Use apps or flashcards to practice vocabulary.
- Review what you've learned regularly.
- Don't be afraid to make mistakes; practice makes perfect!
